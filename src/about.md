# About

Recap Time Squad is an umbrella open-source organization based in the Philippines, founded in 2018 and currently led by its BDFL, [Andrei Jiroh Halili](https://andreijiroh.dev), serving as home for projects like Community Lorebooks and Identity Proofs Toolkit. We're currently operating under Hack Club's non-profit fiscal sponsorship program since 2024.

For an in-depth information about its history, [visit our org profile in the HQ Wiki](https://hq.recaptime.dev/wiki/Recap_Time_Squad).
