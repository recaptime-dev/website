# Hi, we're Recap Time Squad!

We're an all-remote open-source umbrella organization in the Philippines, initially as a home of @ajhalili2006's projects (not only open-source software) since 2018[^1] and currently fiscally hosted under Hack Club's fiscal sponsorship program.

## Featured projects

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :octi-ons-book-24: [Community Lorebooks](https://lorebooks.wiki)

    `.wiki` TLD-based free (sub)domains for digital gardens and community wikis and our hub for documentation and archival-related projects.

-   :material-check-decagram: [Identity Proofs Toolkit](https://idproofstoolkit.recaptime.dev)
    
    Our verification service for the open social web (currently in the Atmosphere) and API service for integrating Keybase, Keyoxide and Keytrace's cryptographic verification in one endpoint minus the horrors of government-issued IDs.

-   :material-radar: [Community Updates Radio (fka Community Radar)](https://updatesradio.lorebooks.wiki)

    Custom feeds and newsletters to share what's on the radar.
</div>
